Privacy

Collection Batch Desk processes catalog CSVs and local thumbnails in your browser. It does not upload catalog data.

Data on your device

The app stores your theme choice. Desk Plus can store a license token, a daily verification result, and a resumable workspace in browser storage. Demo data uses separate browser storage and can be reset from its banner.

Remote thumbnails

Remote image loading is off by default. If you enable it, your browser requests the image hosts named in your CSV. Local image files stay in your browser.

Purchase and license checks

Sociobot and Dodo handle checkout as merchant of record. The app sends only a license token to Sociobot for access checks, at most once each day. It does not send catalog content with that check.

Analytics

This version has no analytics, ads, tracking pixels, cookies, or third-party runtime scripts.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the project repository in the footer.
